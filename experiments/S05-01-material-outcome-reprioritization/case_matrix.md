# S05-01 offline decision-case matrix

These are synthetic, time-bounded **public-input** counterexamples, not reconstructed game decisions or tests of model compliance. M15/M17/M24/M25 are analogy labels from the S04 review, not claims that the exact input text was observed in those months. At each row, only the stated decision-time input is available; later terminal state, score and hidden counters are excluded. `PASS` means the written Skill contract handles the case on inspection, not that an Agent chose well.

| Case | Decision-time public input | Required contract response | Rejected inference | Static result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — M15-like, no new signal | A mitigation was submitted earlier; current public view has no new warning or explicit resolution. | Retain risk-resolution as `UNKNOWN`; do not trigger material reprioritization solely from silence. Reassess only on a plan-changing public signal. | “No warning means resolved.” | PASS |
| B — M17-like, ordinary praise | HR says “good work”; no public statement addresses the prior conflict. | Continue normal evidence-based planning; praise does not erase unresolved risk or force a conservative goal. | “Praise proves safety.” | PASS |
| C — M24-like, severe warning + legal alternatives | Current public warning materially undermines the active growth objective; current choices visibly include continuation and a de-escalation/exit route. | Suspend automatic carry-over of `Priority`; compare the legal paths on known benefit/loss, escalation exposure, opportunity cost and `UNKNOWN`. | “Always continue” or “always exit.” | PASS |
| D — confirmed blowup | A severe consequence is now publicly confirmed; the current level was recently promoted and the prior stance was principled. | Rebuild `Priority` from fresh evidence; compare continuation, reduced exposure and any legal transfer/exit/mitigation. | Promotion, duty or principle proves safety; transfer means failure/demotion. | PASS |
| E — M25-like, retaliation/escalation | Before any terminal outcome, current public feedback shows continuing retaliation/escalation; continuation and lower-exposure paths are currently legal. | Compare both paths' public benefits, losses, escalation exposure, opportunity cost and unknown effects; make no fixed action choice. | Later elimination/score is usable as input; lower exposure is automatically best. | PASS |
| F — ordinary growth | Current public state offers a legal growth opportunity without a material warning, severe consequence or escalating conflict. | Use the normal short-horizon growth comparison; no material checkpoint, automatic conservatism or extra `risk-review` invocation. | Growth is now presumptively inferior. | PASS |
| G — Energy negative control | A legal option visibly offers several benefits; one component appears saturated in the current public state, others do not. No material risk signal. | Do not infer the entire composite option is worthless from one component; retain normal trade-off judgment. This does **not** specify an Energy allocation policy. | One saturated component nullifies every benefit; Energy defect is fixed. | PASS, narrow negative control |

The Skill does not guarantee the model will follow the comparison, choose a different action, improve score/survival, or reduce variance. Exact option effects, hidden risks and counterfactual outcomes remain `UNKNOWN`. `risk-review` retains warning-driven pre-action candidate review; Planning only reopens the strategic objective after a material public signal. The Single Agent remains the only action submitter.
