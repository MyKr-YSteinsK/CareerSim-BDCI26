## Three signal assessment

### Review-to-Result — strongest informational value, still LOW confidence

R1/R2’s C→D sequences had no observed promotion or Output increase by M24. R3’s M6/M12 C feedback was followed by B reviews from M18 and an A at M42/M48; Level advanced at M12/M18/M30/M48. This made it useful to keep the requested outcome open and check later evidence. It did not identify which action produced the review changes, and R3 still scored below 70.

### Opportunity-to-Delivery — useful distinction, weak outcome attribution

R3 did not count project applications as delivery. The M39 automation feasibility work was feasible but more complex than expected, with no explicit completion receipt recovered; an M45 customer demo was planned but no demo result appeared before terminal. M42 feedback praised stable delivery and partner response without attributing it to a named project. This signal prevented a category error but did not provide a complete result chain.

### Affordability — low information in this batch

R3 stayed Wealth-positive throughout (1→27 terminal) with no debt or negative-cash warning; no affordability intervention was called for. R1/R2 also had positive Wealth at termination (7/4) but were eliminated at M24. Thus positive Wealth alone did not distinguish progression or survival in these runs.

## Best / worst and important observations

- **Best:** R3 — completed 48 months, L5, 63.88/B. Later review-to-result sequence and repeated ownership/quality checks are worth retaining as hypotheses. R3 also made a notable confidentiality mistake around M27 by sharing a vague warning about an unconfirmed restructuring; a leader later identified a leak and the player acknowledged it. This is a clear lesson to avoid circulating unverified organizational information.
- **Worst:** R1 by quantitative score (21.36; R2 22.82). Both ended at M24, E/L1/Output 3 after repeated poor reviews, despite positive terminal Wealth. Review feedback and activity did not translate into visible progression during those runs.
- R3’s M21 dependency warning arrived too late after the partner missed; the VP said the warning was late. At M42 a medical leave addressed serious symptoms and delayed one milestone; taking needed leave is not treated as an error, while lack of delivery buffer is a possible planning gap.
- At M45, report preparation/high-intensity/report choices yielded no immediate observed Output increase. This is a direct caution against assuming activity or advertised effects equal delivery.
- The S06 16/16 key-project / 53.83 counterexample and R4’s high report/network counts at 57.90 remain counterevidence to fixed action-frequency rules.

## Hypotheses and Candidate implications

1. **Review request → one named, observable next-window result** — LOW. Supported by the contrast between repeated C/D in R1/R2 and later B/A/progression in R3. Counterevidence: unpaired sessions, multiple simultaneous decisions, and no direct attribution. Worth further public exploration; not ready for Candidate adoption.
2. **Early progression/output gap is a better diagnostic target than activity counts** — LOW. R3’s M6–M24 Level/Output/Wealth remained behind both 75+ references. Counterevidence: opportunity/event variance and no public causal mechanism. Test only in a future authorized run with public commitment/result/review checkpoints.
3. **Keep project opportunity separate from delivered result; treat affordability as a guard** — LOW. R3 had opportunities and feasibility evidence without a verified final delivery receipt; all its Wealth remained positive, while earlier runs’ positive Wealth did not prevent elimination. Counterevidence: M42 feedback praised delivery and partners; no affordability warning occurred. Useful as an evidence discipline, not an action policy.

**Candidate change proposal:** none ready for implementation. If Main later authorizes a strategy-contract review, the smallest candidate-specific question is whether existing Planning can maintain a bounded review-request → observable result → evidence-status note. This should not become a fifth Skill, durable Memory, fixed action ratio, event lookup, or score formula. No formal Candidate change is made here.
